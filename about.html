Arnie Rose

Arnie Rose is a nonfiction author whose work explores identity, human behavior, and the quiet complexities of everyday life. She holds a master’s degree in Literacy Education from Arizona State University and is the author of The Gentle Art of Dying Upside Down and the You Were Never series.

Her writing extends beyond books through a growing body of literary projects. Her collection includes Ode to Home: Letters of the Living, The Good Minute, The Blind Spot Series on Substack, and I Hate Being Human, forthcoming in October 2026. Across these works, she writes about grief, belonging, change, and the ordinary moments that quietly shape a life.

Alongside her literary work, she develops educational resources in project management and supply chain management.
