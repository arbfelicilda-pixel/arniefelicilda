The Keystone House Editorial

Editorial Services for Books That Need More Than Surface-Level Feedback

Thoughtful editorial assessment, manuscript development, and book positioning for nonfiction and literary projects.

We accept a limited number of projects each month.

Editorial Principles

Most editorial feedback is rushed, a manuscript read once and returned with surface notes. A book deserves more careful attention than that, which is why we accept a limited number of projects each month and evaluate every manuscript against five principles.

Clarity
Structure
Reader Experience
Originality
Long-Term Readability

Who This Is For

We work with authors who have a clear book idea, a serious manuscript, and the willingness to revise.

Literary nonfiction
Self-help
Personal development
Reflective nonfiction
Business and leadership books
Memoir with a clear central theme

We do not currently accept academic theses, children's books, poetry-only manuscripts, or projects requiring ghostwriting.

Services

01

Manuscript Development Review

For authors who are still building or restructuring the book.

  • Book concept and central promise
  • Table of contents review
  • Chapter structure
  • Missing or unnecessary sections
  • Repetition and pacing
  • Reader journey
  • Development recommendations

Turnaround 3–5 business days

Suitable for incomplete or early manuscripts

02

Comprehensive Manuscript Review

For authors with a completed draft who need an in-depth editorial assessment.

  • Full manuscript assessment
  • Chapter-by-chapter notes
  • Structure and flow
  • Voice and clarity
  • Reader engagement
  • Editorial scorecard
  • Priority revision plan

Turnaround 5–10 business days

Suitable for completed manuscripts

03

Cover-to-Cover Review

For authors preparing to publish or relaunch a book.

  • Title and subtitle testing
  • Cover assessment
  • Book description and back cover copy
  • Author biography
  • Interior sample pages
  • Reader positioning

Turnaround 3–5 business days

Suitable for pre-publication or relaunch

Our Editorial Process

01

Submit Your Project

Complete the inquiry form below. Your email opens automatically, pre-filled and ready to send.

02

Editorial Fit Review

Every project is reviewed for editorial fit, manuscript readiness, genre alignment, scope, and availability.

03

Invitation and Recommendation

Accepted projects receive a private invitation with the recommended service, scope, timeline, and price.

04

Booking and Submission

Once accepted, the manuscript is submitted in full and the project is officially booked.

05

Editorial Review and Delivery

The completed editorial report and recommendations are delivered by the agreed date.

Project Acceptance. To maintain the quality and depth of our editorial work, we accept a limited number of projects. Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee acceptance.

Projects are selected based on editorial fit, manuscript readiness, scope, genre, timeline, and current availability. If we believe another service, editor, or stage of development would better serve the project, we will say so directly.
