Arnie Rose

Arnie Rose is a nonfiction author whose work explores identity, human behavior, and the quiet complexities of everyday life. She is the author of The Gentle Art of Dying Upside Down and the You Were Never series. Her recent literary collections, including Ode to Home: Letters of the Living and The Good Minute, serve as companion works to her new book. Beyond her literary work, she develops educational resources in project management and supply chain management.
