The Author

"You've been doing everything right. Nothing's moving. That's not a motivation problem. That's a structure problem."

Arnie Rose Felicilda, M.A.Ed. is the founder of Keystone Books and the author of the You Were Never Series. She spent over ten years in supply chain leadership across the Philippines and China before burnout stopped her cold. The series came from that unraveling.

She teaches supply chain management and project management to operations managers and team leads who were handed responsibility without the systems to handle it. They just need systems that work.
