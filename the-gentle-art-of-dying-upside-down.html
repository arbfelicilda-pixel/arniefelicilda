About the Book

You don't always lose a life all at once. What once felt like belonging begins to feel like endurance.

Sooner or later, every life asks:
How do we begin again?

The Gentle Art of Dying Upside Down is for anyone learning to let go of a life they once believed would last forever. It explores grief, identity, change, and the quiet courage it takes to begin again—not by forgetting the past, but by learning how to carry it differently.

This is not a book of quick answers or easy advice. It is a thoughtful companion for seasons of transition, when loss leaves questions behind and the next step is not yet clear. If you are finding your way through change, searching for yourself again, or standing at the beginning of a new chapter, these pages were written for you.

About the Author

Arnie Rose is a nonfiction author whose work explores identity, human behavior, and the quiet complexities of everyday life. She holds a master's degree in Literacy Education from Arizona State University and is the author of The Gentle Art of Dying Upside Down and the You Were Never series. Her writing extends beyond books through a growing body of literary projects, including Ode to Home: Letters of the Living, The Good Minute, The Blind Spot Series on Substack, and I Hate Being Human, forthcoming in October 2026. Alongside her literary work, she develops educational resources in project management and supply chain management.
